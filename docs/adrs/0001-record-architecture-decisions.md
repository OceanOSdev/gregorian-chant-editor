# 1. Record architecture decisions

Date: 2026-07-26

## Status

Accepted

## Context

We need to record the architectural decisions made on this project.

## Decision

We will use Architecture Decision Records, as
[described by Michael Nygard](https://cognitect.com/blog/2011/11/15/documenting-architecture-decisions).

## Consequences

### Positive

- Important architectural rationale is retained alongside the code.
- Superseded decisions remain discoverable.
- Contributors have a consistent place to discuss durable architectural
  choices.

### Negative

- Contributors must maintain the ADR log and keep its links accurate.
- Architectural changes may require a new or superseding record.
